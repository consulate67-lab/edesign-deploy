e-Invoice template designer for XRechnung, Peppol and EN 16931

Updated: 15 October 2026 · 4 min read

Structured e-invoices such as XRechnung and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 are XML files. Machines read them easily, people do not. An XSLT stylesheet turns that XML into a clear, branded invoice your customers and accountants can read, approve and print. edXdocu lets you design that layout without writing a line of XSLT.

Why your e-invoices need a readable layout

  • Approval workflows: Accounts-payable teams still look at the invoice before paying it.
  • Branding: Your logo, colours and bank details appear on every invoice, wherever it is opened.
  • Fewer queries: Clear totals, VAT breakdown, payment terms and IBAN mean faster payment.
  • Archiving and print: A consistent A4 view for audits and customers who still print.

FAQ

Why does an XRechnung or Peppol invoice need a template?

The invoice itself is pure XML. An XSLT stylesheet turns it into a readable, branded view for approval, printing and archiving.

Do I need to know XSLT?

No. You customise a ready-made template in a drag-and-drop editor; the XSLT is generated for you.

Is edXdocu an e-invoicing access point?

No. edXdocu designs the invoice layout. Sending and receiving invoices is done by your ERP or Peppol access point.
